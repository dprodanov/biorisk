Laboratory biosafety traditionally uses categorical Risk Groups and checklists
that cannot quantify intervention effectiveness. This paper specifies a Bayesian
Markov chain framework combining WHO Risk Group priors with five-state escalation
from normal operation to disaster, reporting risk on a log-risk scale analogous
to decibels.
Training, maintenance, and inspection effects decompose cleanly via a sparse
weight matrix. Bayesian updating from near-misses, light incidents, and disasters
recovers composite risk metrics even with non-identifiable parameters.
The framework operationalises ISO 35001:2019 and WHO LBM4, transforming static
compliance into quantitative decision support.
